GripCheck

Team project, September 2026 to now

A low-cost 3D-printed grip dynamometer. My part is the test software: 5 force-time metrics against published norms, and a check that catches bad trials.

99% vs 92% for the bad-trial check against hand-tuned rules, on simulated curves. Readings stay datasheet-scaled until calibration is done.

Python, Flask, Arduino, scikit-learn, SciPy
